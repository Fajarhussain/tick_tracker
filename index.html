Education & species identification guide

Ticks are small arachnids that feed on the blood of mammals and birds. In the UK, some species can pass on diseases such as Lyme disease. The guide below is built from the species in our sightings data.

Prevention and removal tips

  • Check yourself and your pets: run your hands through your pet's fur after walks in grass or woodland, and check yourself and children too.
  • Use approved treatments: ask your vet about tick spot-ons, collars or tablets for pets.
  • Cover up outdoors: wear long trousers and use repellent when walking through long grass or bracken.
  • Remove ticks safely: use a tick remover or fine-tipped tweezers and pull steadily, close to the skin. Then clean the bite.
  • Tidy your garden: keep grass short and clear leaf litter where ticks hide.
  • Watch for symptoms: if you or your pet become unwell after a bite, or a rash spreads, contact a doctor or vet.
